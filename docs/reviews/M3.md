## Summary verdict

M2's math is excellent, and M3 is a real, playable arrangement DAW skeleton with a convincing Live look. Measured highlights:
- **Tuning core**
  - The linear form matches `buildFullScale` over 11.5 M degree evaluations, max error 1.8e-11 ¢. The sweep covered:
    - n 2–24, every rotation;
    - periods including 1901.955 ¢ and 240 ¢;
    - 1–3 periods per equave;
    - stretch ±20 ¢;
    - negative and above-period generators;
    - degrees well outside one period.
  - float32 Hz error is ≤ 8.7e-5 ¢.
  - EDO, Scala/.kbm, JI utilities and the virtual fundamental all match independent references.
  - MOS fixture coverage is good.
- **Timeline and lowering**
  - Note onsets land on the exact sample of an independent `ticksToSamples` model. Tested cases:
    - tempo ramps and odd ticks;
    - looped clips and content offsets;
    - notes cut at the clip end.
  - Pitches (31-EDO, 5L2s, 13ed3, ratios, cents) are within 1e-4 ¢ of the SPEC formulas.
  - The pan and gain laws are within 0.01 dB.
  - Automation follows the Q39 curve law exactly.
  - About 50 malformed project files are all rejected cleanly.
- **UI**
  - Partial redraw is clean.
  - The arrangement, piano roll and chart are well inside their budgets (0.34 / 0.36 / 0.08 ms).
  - Idle CPU is 0.024 %; first frame 202 ms.
  - HiDPI is clean at 1.25–2.0.
- **Previous reviews**
  - All 2 Critical and 6 High findings of M1 are fixed. All M1 probes pass on both backends, and the JIT and interpreter are bit-identical.

The problems cluster in four places:

1. **The playback link is not ready for real projects.**
   - The idle gate reads a freed unit (C1).
   - The mixer that lowering generates costs about 51 ns per track × return per sample, even when silent. 64 tracks with 8 returns needs 140 % of a core, so it cannot play (H1).
   - Lowering builds each track from **generated text**. Adding one return rewrites every track's patch, so every unit recompiles and crossfades and every state key changes (H2).
   - This text-lowering design is the main architectural risk for M4's devices and the project patch view.
2. **The tuning signal's coverage has holes.** The kernel is right, but the wiring around it isn't:
   - at an exact region boundary (720 ¢, 400 ¢, 600 ¢: round numbers users type) the node and the static pitch disagree by whole steps (H3);
   - clip overrides, Ratio-on-Degree anchors and Cents notes ignore tuning automation (H4);
   - a live tuning edit cuts held notes that keep static pitches (H5).

   SPEC §3.2's "automating Tuning A re-voices every track/clip that follows A" holds only for Degree notes in clips that inherit their track's tuning.
3. **Much of the microtonal functionality is unreachable from the UI (H7).** There is:
   - no way to add, rename or remove a main tuning;
   - no way to set the reference pitch, equave or stretch;
   - no way to enter a Ratio note or convert a note's identity;
   - no ratio label on Ratio notes;
   - no menu bar, so Save, Open and Preferences are hotkey-only.

   The model and edit operations exist and are tested; the UI doesn't expose them.
4. **M4's prerequisites aren't in place (H6).**
   - The stdlib has no filters or envelopes (T1b.3 not started).
   - pqdaw runs interpreter-only: T1.x-arch2 / T1b.5 are unmerged.
   - T1.x-text-ids is unmerged.
   - T1.x-silence is not started.
   - The document has no device model.

Process:
- Acceptance criteria were met as written almost everywhere, and the workers' `-Full` dev + release discipline has visibly improved.
- Several orchestrator merge checks still ran subsets of the tests (M1 L12), most recently T3.2-fix1 with dev `-Full ui|views|app`.
- A few documented claims are wrong:
  - Q95.2: the per-send cost is "one stereo multiply and one add";
  - Q95.6: tuning lanes are not lowered (they are, since T2.6-fix1);
  - Q105.4's error bound;
  - Q105.3: the tables are "a few KB" (they reach 509 KiB at n = 60).

## UI and aesthetic

Compared with the user's Live 12 Arrangement screenshot, region by region, and every golden at 1.0 and 1.25–2.0. These were local comparisons only.

**What works.**
- The palette is sampled well. Lanes, headers, the ruler and the detail panel all match Live's `#26383F`, and the track colours, amber activators, teal value fills and red arm read as Live.
- Headers on the right, with the name strip in the track colour and the mixer section beside it, look right at a glance, and so do clip bodies with note previews, the overview strip, the ruler with the loop brace and the min:sec ruler.
- The microtonal additions fit the Live idiom rather than fighting it:
  - the tuning chip under each track name;
  - Layout / EDO Grid / Generator rows in the clip box;
  - proportional MOS rows with hardness-blended key colours.
- The MOS chart and circle are faithful, legible ports of mos-explorer.
- HiDPI is crisp at every scale tested.

**What doesn't (concrete changes, most valuable first):**

1. **Discoverability (H7).**
   - There's no menu bar, so a new user can't find Save or Preferences, and the tuning features are behind one chip and Ctrl+Shift+W. Add Live's menu bar row (File, Edit, Create, View, Options, Help) at the top. It costs one 19 px row.
   - "Create" should hold Insert MIDI Track, Insert Return Track and **New Main Tuning**. "Options" should hold Preferences.
   - The info view (bottom-left, 200 px) is blank until hover. Live shows a default hint; show "Hover over anything for help · Ctrl+Shift+W: MOS wheel · …".
2. **Make JI visible in the piano roll.**
   - Show ratio labels on Ratio notes ("7/4") and harmonic numbers on Harmonic notes, in the note's top-left when the note is ≥ 24 px wide.
   - Move the harmonic guide numbers off the notes into a right-hand strip (M18).
   - Add a row-label mode: degree, cents, ratio for JI Scala scales (a Just major's rows should read 1/1 9/8 5/4 4/3 3/2 5/3 15/8, not 0–6), or note name.
   - Show the hovered row's exact pitch (cents, Hz, nearest ratio, deviation from the overlay EDO) in the info view. This is what makes a microtonal roll usable.
3. **Control bar.**
   - It is `#26383F` with dark buttons. Live's transport bar is the window colour `#16282E` with panel-coloured fields (Q107.1, verified by sampling).
   - It is also very sparse: tempo, signature, position, transport, loop, loop start/length and a grid label.
   - Do Q107.1 option B. Add the CPU meter (the data exists in `AudioPrefsPort`), a metronome placeholder and a tuning readout of the selected track's tuning (a coloured chip like the header's). The last one makes the "tunings are first class" idea visible at the top of the window.
4. **Panel separation.**
   - Live draws each area (browser, arrangement, detail, info) as a panel on the darker `#16282E` window, with about 6 px gutters. pqdaw butts panels together with 1 px lines, so the screen reads as one flat slab.
   - Add theme tokens for a gutter and a panel inset. This is a layout change in `shell/`, not just data.
5. **Detail view.**
   - It takes about 35 % of the height at start and says only "No clip selected".
   - Live opens the detail view when you double-click a clip. Start with it collapsed (Ctrl+Alt+L shows it), or give it Live's "Drop … here" hint text.
6. **Typography.**
   - Inter at 12/13 px (bold 13 for track names) is visibly larger and heavier than Live's 11 px Ableton Sans with 7 px caps. The headers look chunky beside Live's.
   - Take Q107 option B: 11 px roles plus a second glyph-atlas page, which is cheap.
   - Use SemiBold, not Bold, for track names.
7. **MOS wheel popover.**
   - At 920 × 580 it covers the very piano roll whose live morph it is meant to show (`mos-wheel-popover.png`).
   - Make it dockable in the detail area's left half (the clip box plus the info view are 350 px wide; the circle alone fits at about 340 px), or let the popover be dragged and remember its position.
   - Also make the wheel audible while dragging for main-tuning targets. Since T2.6-fix1 a generator change is a param write, so Q83.2's reason for preview-only no longer applies.
8. **Mixer view.**
   - The fader is a 2 px line with a small cap. Live's mixer fader is a wider track with a triangle and a dB scale.
   - The tuning chip truncates ("12-E…"), and the pan marker strikes through the text (L17).
   - The view replaces the arrangement (Q89.8). Live 12 shows the mixer under the tracks; docking it is worth the shell change.
9. **Collisions** (L18, M18): the chart labels, the "132L 11s" size column, sliver-row key labels and the harmonic labels. Add a single text-collision helper in `views/common`.
10. **Hotkeys vs Live 12** (from memory of the manual):
    - matching: transport, undo/redo, file, Shift+Tab, Ctrl+Alt+L/B/M, Ctrl+1–5, B, Z/X, ±, Ctrl+G/Shift+G, Ctrl+R, 0, A, Ctrl+E/J/D/L/I, Ctrl+Shift+D/M/Delete, Ctrl+U, Up/Down/Shift-Up/Down;
    - inverted: Ctrl+T vs Ctrl+Shift+T (L15);
    - missing: the arrow keys in the arrangement (move the insert marker, select tracks), Home/End, Ctrl+F (browser search, M5);
    - not applicable: Tab (Session/Arrangement switch, no Session View).

    Add arrow-key navigation in the arrangement and the roll (Left/Right move the insert marker by a grid step).
